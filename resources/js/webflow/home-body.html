Any Questions? I’ve got the answers.

Does Boardmasters cover Nursing and other board exam tracks?
Yes. Boardmasters provides comprehensive, up-to-date practice material for six review tracks: Nursing (PNLE), Teacher Education (LET), Criminology (CLE), Civil Engineering (CELE), Midwifery (MLE), and Radiologic Technology (RTLE).
How is Boardmasters better than printed reviewers or books?
Boardmasters provides real-time, objective performance analysis that paper reviewers cannot. It tracks your results, pinpoints weak topics by subject and sub-topic, and prevents you from wasting time on concepts you have already mastered.
Is an internet connection required to use the app?
An internet connection is required for initial login, syncing progress, and downloading updated question sets. Many core review and practice test functionalities can be accessed offline once the content has been fully loaded.
How often is the question bank updated?
Our content team, composed of licensed professionals and board topnotchers, continuously reviews and updates the question bank and solutions to align with current board exam coverage and guidelines.
How realistic are the Mock Board Exams in terms of difficulty and format?
Our mock board exams are meticulously timed and weighted to match the difficulty curve, structure, and pacing of actual board exams, providing a practical readiness benchmark.
When should I integrate Boardmasters into my review schedule?
We recommend integrating Boardmasters as early as possible. The sooner you start tracking your performance objectively, the more time you have to identify and address weaknesses, making your overall review more efficient.
